## How the convergence zone is defined (important)

The peg is **held** at the offset and the insertion controller works **from that handoff point**
(it spirals/servos around where the peg was handed off, not around the socket axis). This is the
**passive** capture region — the funnel + local search, *without* crediting the controller's
socket-pose re-centering. It is a deliberately conservative (lower-bound) convergence zone; it is
§2.1-clean (the controller still only sees socket pose + EE proprioception + F/T).

## Results — c=2.0, L0, 4 azimuths/cell

Capture rate vs (offset r, tilt θ), averaged over azimuth:

| θ\r | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0° | 100 | 100 | 75 | 50 | 25 | 25 |
| 2° | 100 | 100 | 50 | 75 | 25 | 25 |
| 4° | 75 | 75 | 50 | 25 | 25 | 0 |
| 6° | 100 | 50 | 50 | 75 | 25 | 25 |
| 8° | 25 | 0 | 25 | 50 | 50 | 0 |

- **Lateral capture radius (θ≈0, ≥50%): ~3 mm.** Capture is ~100% for r≤1 mm and decays with both
  offset and tilt. (Cell noise is the 4-azimuth quantization → 0/25/50/75/100%; more azimuths/trials
  would smooth it.)

## Overlay — the paper's validation argument

Projecting the zone (r≤3 mm disk) onto the (dx,dy) handoff plane and overlaying the **measured**
M5 handoff errors (c=2.0/L1, 50 episodes): **P(handoff error ∈ passive zone) = 52%**, yet M5
measured **100%** insertion success.

**Finding.** The pick-side terminal-error distribution straddles the *passive* convergence-zone
boundary (~half inside). The deployed controller nonetheless inserts 100% — so the **active
socket-pose re-centering (+ spiral) recovers the handoff errors that fall outside the passive funnel
zone**. E4 thus quantifies the active-control margin: passive funnel/compliance alone would capture
~52% of handoffs; the rule-based controller's active search closes the gap to 100%. As clearance
tightens (future c≤1.0 runs) the zone shrinks and this margin is where the success curve will bend.

## Reproduce

```bash
unset DISPLAY
/isaac-sim/python.sh scripts/bdash/run_convergence_zone.py --policy_type bdash_scripted \
    --num_envs 8 --seed 0 --r_mm 0 1 2 3 4 5 --az_deg 0 90 180 270 --tilt_deg 0 2 4 6 8 \
    --max_steps 1250 --out results/bdash/e4_convergence_c2.csv \
    bdash_pick_insert --clearance 2.0 --level L0
/isaac-sim/python.sh scripts/bdash/make_e4_figure.py \
    --conv_csv results/bdash/e4_convergence_c2.csv --out docs/images/bdash/e4_convergence_c2.png
```
