## Results — c=2.0, 50 trials/cell, seed 0

| level | success | 95% CI | insert force median | handoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L0 (canonical) | **50/50 (100%)** | [0.93, 1.0] | 8.6 N | 50/50 |
| L1 (pose/yaw) | **50/50 (100%)** | [0.93, 1.0] | 0.21 N | 50/50 |
| L2 (+lighting) | **49/50 (98%)** | [0.90, 1.0] | 0.60 N | 50/50 |
| L3 (+texture/distractors) | **49/50 (98%)** | [0.90, 1.0] | 0.60 N | 50/50 |

**Headline finding — scene-diversity invariance.** The rule-based controller holds 98–100% flat
across L0→L3. This is expected and is a paper point: the scripted expert is **privileged** (it does
not perceive), so lighting / texture / distractors cannot affect it. The contrast — the VLA front-end
degrading on L2/L3 — is **E3** (after the M8 vision baseline / a trained VLA), not here. Insertion
contact force stays single-digit at every level; `handoff` fires 50/50 everywhere.

## Remaining — c ≤ 1.0 (diagnosed; needs a controller enhancement, not tuning)

At c=1.0 the controller cliffs to **0/24** (a hard cliff from 100% at c=2.0). Root cause, from the
debug trace: the peg descends through the 3 mm funnel and **wedges at the funnel→bore rim at depth
≈2.6 mm, lat ≈0.8 mm** — for a 1 mm radial clearance the tip edge sits ~0.2 mm from the bore wall and
catches. Because the §2.1-compliant controller is a **stiff position-controlled EE** (it commands the
tip position; it does not passively comply), it can't free a mechanically wedged peg, and the
full-retract retry resets the spiral each time.

**Config tuning is the wrong tool and makes it worse** — swept spiral-engage/RCC (force 13→17 N),
then gentler-press + longer-spiral (force 27 N, **165 N max** jam): all 0/24. Reverted; c=2.0 back to
**24/24**.

**What it actually needs (next task, #13):** a wedge-recovery behavior — when stuck near the rim
(0 < depth < capture, no depth progress), do a **small lift to unload the wedge + lateral re-search**,
then re-press (a "hop-and-search"), instead of the full retract; or per-axis compliance during PRESS.
This is a controller change, scoped but non-trivial, with c=2.0-regression risk to watch.

**Note for the paper:** the cliff is itself a valid E2 result — it quantifies the rule-based
controller's clearance limit (works at c=2.0, fails at c≤1.0 without active compliance). A *graded*
curve needs the enhancement above. **Decision pending: implement the enhancement, or report the cliff.**
