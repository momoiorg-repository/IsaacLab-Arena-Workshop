# M11 — Insertion diagnosis: the residual is grasp quality, not the controller ✅

Status: **the closed-loop insertion gap is fully localized.** After M10's frozen-frames fix lifted the
VLA to 20% end-to-end, this milestone isolates *why* only 4/15 clean handoffs convert. The answer — by
controlled isolation, not inference — is **grasp quality**: the VLA holds the peg cocked **in the
gripper** (~8° / ~9 mm), and the §2.1 peg-pose-blind insertion controller provably cannot see or
correct that. The controller itself is not the bottleneck (98% from a clean handoff). This is a hard
*architectural* limiter of the hierarchy, and it cleanly motivates the next VLA iteration.

> Supersedes M10 §6's "the InsertionController is jamming" reading.

---

## 1. The funnel (L1, c=2.0, n=20, single seed)

| stage | v6-recovery |
|---|---:|
| grasp | 17/20 (85%) |
| clean handoff | 15/20 (75%) |
| **insert (success)** | **4/20 (20%)**, CI95 [0.081, 0.416] |

The question: 15 clean handoffs, only 4 inserts. Where do the other 11 go, and is it the controller or
the front-end?

## 2. Isolation 1 — the controller is fine (98% from a clean handoff)

Running the **scripted** expert (deterministic pick → handoff → the *same* rule `InsertionController`,
batched, no cameras, no VLA):

| policy (c2.0/L1, n=50) | insert | f_ins |
|---|---:|---:|
| **vdash_scripted** | **49/50 = 98%**, CI95 [0.895, 0.997] | 0.6 N |

So the controller converts a good handoff almost always. The 20% is not a generic controller
deficiency.

## 3. Isolation 2 — the controller is robust to *handoff pose*, and RCC hurts

We map the **convergence basin** with a new harness mode (`run_convergence_zone.py --recover`): deliver
the peg to a controlled lateral offset `r` and tilt `θ`, then release so the controller recenters on
the true axis (the faithful basin), vs the old "held" map (peg pinned at the offset = static tolerance).

| basin (c2.0, 117 cells) | overall | by radius | by tilt |
|---|---:|---|---|
| held (static tolerance) | 37% | collapses past ~2 mm | cliff at 8° |
| **recovery (faithful)** | **83%**, CI95 [0.760, 0.894] | **flat ~85% to 5 mm** | 100→70% (8°) |

The real controller **recovers lateral handoff offset to ≥5 mm and tolerates tilt to ~8°**. So neither
the VLA's handoff offset (\|dxy\| ~6.5 mm) nor handoff tilt (mean 5.6°, median 4.9°, max 15.7°) *alone*
predicts 20% — the basin predicts ~75–85% at those levels. And the one tilt-correction the controller
has, **RCC rotational compliance, makes it worse** (recovery basin 83% → 29% at gain 0.01) — which is
why the config ships it off.

## 4. Isolation 3 — the cause is the in-gripper grasp (measured)

Instrumenting the *in-gripper* pose at handoff (peg axis vs the gripper/`ee_frame` axis the controller's
`tip_estimate` assumes; lateral offset of the peg from that axis):

| in-gripper at handoff | scripted (98%) | VLA v6 (20%) |
|---|---|---|
| **tilt** | 0.1–0.6° | **3.3–11.8° (mean ~8°)** |
| **lateral** | 0.7–0.8 mm | **5.4–13.1 mm (mean ~9 mm)** |

The VLA grasps the peg **crooked**. Because the grasp is rigid, the controller's nominal tip-estimate is
then wrong by ~`grip_offset·sin 8° + 9 mm ≈ 10–17 mm` — far outside the 2 mm clearance and 8 mm spiral
reach. It servos the wrong point → presses the peg in canted → jam (failures: final tilt mean 32° /
median 19° / max 83°). The 4 successes are the near-nominal grasps.

## 5. Isolation 4 — it is unrecoverable downstream (the §2.1 limiter)

A controlled in-gripper offset injected into the *scripted* pick (test-bed) reproduces the failure and
shows nothing in the controller's reach fixes it:

| c2.0/L1, n=20, 8 mm in-gripper offset | insert |
|---|---:|
| baseline (assumed tip) | 0/20 |
| + wider spiral (14 mm) | 0/20 |
| + slow/wide spiral | 0/20 |
| + RCC translational compliance | 0/20 |
| **+ controller given the *true* tip (cheat, violates §2.1)** | **11/20 = 55%** |

The reason every search fails: the controller's capture/seat logic is computed from the **assumed** tip
(`depth = mouth_z − assumed_tip_z`), so when the true tip is 9 mm away it cannot even *detect* the true
tip entering the bore. Only feeding it the true tip recovers insertion — and even then caps at 55%,
because the peg is still physically held off-axis. **A §2.1 peg-pose-blind controller cannot correct an
in-gripper grasp error.** A reseat primitive is additionally blocked by the binary gripper (open = drop,
close = rigid; no compliant partial-open).

## 5.6. E2 clearance floor — precision-limited, not a controller gap

Sweeping clearance (scripted, n=50, `figs/vdash/e2_clearance.png`) gives a **cliff, not a curve**:
c=2.0 → 100% (L0) / 98% (L1); **c≤1.0 → 0% at every level**. The cliff is **not** a config artifact —
c=1.0 re-run with `use_hop_search` ON is still **0/50** (f_ins 16–31 N). The cause is precision: even
the scripted handoff's ~4 mm lateral exceeds what fits a ≤1 mm hole. So the classical baseline has a
**~2 mm clearance floor set by handoff precision** — the same precision axis that limits the VLA. Front-
end precision is the binding constraint at *every* clearance, reinforcing §6.

## 6. Conclusion

The hierarchy's residual is **front-end grasp quality**, and §2.1 makes it **architecturally
unfixable downstream**:

```
frozen-frames fix (M10)  →  VLA perceives the peg (5 mm tracking), grasps 85%, hands off 75%
classical control        →  inserts a clean handoff at 98%; basin tolerates 5 mm / 8° of handoff error
BUT the VLA grasp is cocked ~8°/9 mm in the gripper
the §2.1 peg-blind controller cannot see it  →  4/15 convert (20% end-to-end)
```

This is a positive result for the paper's thesis (precision = classical control) *and* a precise,
measured motivation for the next VLA iteration: **grasp quality** (not grasp rate, which is fine at 85%)
must be improved in the front-end. Per the project scope that is deferred to the next VLA training
(request v3 / Sept), not patched in the controller.

## 8. Caveats (per the paper-claims audit)

- All numbers **single seed (0)**; the VLA 20% carries the ~5–6% image-aug variance (multi-seed not run).
- Use **L1** as the scripted headline — **L0 is unstable run-to-run** (54–100% at seed 0 across code/config
  revisions); do not cite a single L0 number.
- The scripted/M8 paths switch control on the internal `pick_finished` flag; **predicate switching** is
  the **VLA** path only, and the §3.4 predicate is evaluated on ground-truth (no uprightness term).
- No **c3.0** socket ships (the series is {2.0, 1.0, 0.5, 0.25}); a wider socket must be generated for a
  looser-clearance point.
- The cheat is a **diagnostic only** (it violates §2.1) — it is not a proposed solution and is not in the
  committed control path.
